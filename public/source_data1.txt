SkillByte 02G – Real-World APs: From Steps to Savings
🎯 Learning Objective:
 Use the nth term formula aₙ = a + (n – 1)·d to solve real-life Arithmetic Progression problems like savings, steps, deliveries, and more!

Step 1: Conceptual Nudge – “Your Life Runs on Patterns”
👟 Your fitness tracker shows:
 • Day 1 = 5,000 steps
 • Day 2 = 5,300
 • Day 3 = 5,600...
You’re adding +300 steps daily — that’s an AP ✅
Want to know:
 📈 Steps on Day 10?
 🎯 When you’ll hit 10,000 steps?
Use the formula:
 👉 aₙ = a + (n – 1)·d
This is how your progress becomes predictable 🔍

Step 2: Quiz Time – NCERT Style
📘 Q. A child saves ₹500 at age 5, increasing by ₹100 each year. What’s the saving at age 15?
Choose:
 ► A) ₹1,400
 ► B) ₹1,500
 ► C) ₹1,600
✅ Correct Answer: B) ₹1,500
📐 Let’s solve:
• a = ₹500
 • d = ₹100
 • n = 11 (from age 5 to 15)
a₁₁ = 500 + 10×100 = ✅ ₹1,500
❌ Picked A or C?
 Double-check n — it’s 11 years, not 10!

Step 3: Explain It – AP Formula in the Real World
💡 Real-life APs are everywhere:
• a = Starting value
 • d = Daily/weekly/monthly change
 • n = Position you want to predict
📌 Use:
 👉 aₙ = a + (n – 1)·d
📘 CBSE Marking Tip:
 • Formula = 1 mark
 • Substitution = 1 mark
 • Final answer = 1 mark ✅

Step 4: Challenge – CBSE Application Question
📘 Q. Pizza delivery charges ₹50 for 1st km and ₹15 per extra km. What’s the cost for 12 km?
Choose:
 ► A) ₹215
 ► B) ₹200
 ► C) ₹185
✅ Correct Answer: A) ₹215
📐 Let’s solve:
• a = ₹50
 • d = ₹15
 • For 12th km = 11 additional charges
a₁₂ = 50 + 11×15 = ✅ ₹215
❌ Picked B or C?
 You may have skipped the 1st km’s special price.
