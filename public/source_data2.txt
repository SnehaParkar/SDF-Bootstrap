🧠 SkillByte 02E – 3/4-Term APs: The Symmetry Shortcut
🎯 Learning Objective:
 Solve 3-term and 4-term AP problems using symmetry and algebra shortcuts — even for sum, GP, or square-based questions.

Step 1: Conceptual Nudge – “Prize Money Logic”
🏆 Your school gives prizes in an AP:
• 2nd Prize = ₹x
 • 1st Prize = ₹x + d
 • Special Prize = ₹x + 2d
Notice the equal jumps?
 That’s the power of symmetry in APs!
📌 Setup tricks:
• 3 terms → (x − d), x, (x + d)
 • 4 terms → (x − 3d), (x − d), (x + d), (x + 3d)
This makes solving way easier 🧠

Step 2: Quiz Time – CBSE PYQ Style
📘 CBSE 2023 Question:
 Q. Three numbers in AP sum to 24.
 If 1, 2, and 6 are added to them respectively, the result forms a GP. Find the numbers.
Choose:
 ► A) 4, 8, 12
 ► B) 6, 8, 10
 ► C) 3, 8, 13
✅ Correct Answer: B) 6, 8, 10
📐 Let’s solve:
• Let AP = (x − d), x, (x + d)
 • Sum: 3x = 24 ⇒ x = 8
 • New sequence = (8 − d) + 1, 8 + 2, (8 + d) + 6
 → (9 − d), 10, (14 + d)
• For GP: middle² = first × third
 → 10² = (9 − d)(14 + d)
 → 100 = 126 + 5d − d²
 → Solve: d² − 5d − 26 = 0
 → d = ±2 → Try d = 2 ⇒ AP = 6, 8, 10 ✅
❌ A and C?
 Plug and test — they won’t satisfy GP logic.

Step 3: Explain It – Symmetry Is Your Shortcut
🧠 Why use symmetry?
• Middle term in 3-term AP = average
 • Outer terms in 4-term AP = mirror images
🎯 Steps for any 3/4 term AP:
1️⃣ Set up using (x − d), x, (x + d)
 2️⃣ Use sum / square / GP / condition
 3️⃣ Solve for x and d
🧠 CBSE Tip: Start with correct format — it makes everything easier.

Step 4: Challenge – 4-Term Square Sum Puzzle
📘 Q. Four numbers in AP sum to 20.
 Their squares sum to 120. What are the numbers?
Choose:
 ► A) 1, 3, 5, 11
 ► B) 2, 4, 6, 8
 ► C) 1, 4, 7, 8
✅ Correct Answer: B) 2, 4, 6, 8
📐 Let’s solve:
• Let terms = (x − 3d), (x − d), (x + d), (x + 3d)
 • Sum = 4x = 20 ⇒ x = 5
Squares:
 (5 − 3d)² + (5 − d)² + (5 + d)² + (5 + 3d)²
 → Add: 4×25 + 20d² = 120
 → 100 + 20d² = 120
 → d² = 1 ⇒ d = ±1
 → Try x = 5, d = 1 ⇒ AP = 2, 4, 6, 8 ✅
❌ A or C?
 They either don’t sum to 20 or fail square sum = 120
